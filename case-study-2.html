HealthTrack Patient Portal

Company HealthTrack
Role Senior UX Design Manager
Duration 14 months
Team 8 designers, 12 engineers

The Challenge

HealthTrack's patient portal served over 2 million users, but the mobile experience was built as a responsive wrapper around the desktop site. Patients struggled with core tasks like booking appointments, viewing test results, and messaging their care team. The appointment completion rate sat at just 52%, and the app had a 2.8-star rating.

The organisation needed a mobile-first redesign that would improve health outcomes by reducing friction in patient-provider communication.

Research & Discovery

I led a comprehensive research programme to understand patient needs:

  • Diary studies with 24 patients over 4 weeks to understand real-world usage patterns and pain points
  • Contextual interviews with patients across age groups, technical comfort levels, and health conditions
  • Analytics deep-dive revealing that 73% of drop-offs occurred during the appointment booking flow
  • Accessibility audit that uncovered 48 WCAG violations in the existing experience

Design Process

Based on research insights, I guided the team through a focused redesign:

  • Simplified booking flow: Reduced appointment booking from 7 steps to 3 by pre-filling known patient data and using intelligent provider suggestions
  • Task-oriented dashboard: Replaced the information-dense home screen with a prioritised task list showing upcoming appointments, pending actions, and new results
  • Accessible by default: Built to WCAG 2.1 AA with support for Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and high-contrast mode
  • Offline-capable messaging: Patients could draft messages to their care team even without connectivity, with automatic sync when online

Results

+34% Appointment completion rate
4.6★ App store rating (from 2.8)
+91% NPS improvement (32→61)
0 Critical a11y violations

Reflection

This project reinforced my belief that accessibility and usability are the same goal. By designing for patients with the most constraints — low vision, limited dexterity, low digital literacy — we created a better experience for everyone. The research investment upfront was significant, but it gave us the confidence to make bold simplifications that moved the metrics.
