Atlassian System of Work

Company Atlassian
Role Lead Designer
Duration Apr 2024 – Present
Scope All Atlassian products

The Challenge

Atlassian's product suite has grown significantly — from Jira and Confluence to include Loom, Trello, Bitbucket, Rovo, and more. With over 300,000 customers and offices across San Francisco, Mountain View, Austin, Bangalore, Tokyo, Manila, Sydney, and Amsterdam, the products enable teams to work together effectively across today's shifting workplace.

As the suite expanded through both organic growth and acquisitions, navigation patterns diverged across products. Users switching between tools encountered different navigation paradigms, creating cognitive overhead and reducing the sense of a unified platform. The challenge: create a cohesive navigation experience that ties the entire Atlassian ecosystem together — without disrupting the deeply embedded workflows that millions rely on daily.

My Role

As Lead Designer for the System of Work initiative, I own the end-to-end design of the navigation experience that connects all Atlassian products. This includes:

  • Defining the navigation framework and interaction patterns that will scale across the full product suite
  • Collaborating with product designers embedded in each product to ensure the navigation integrates seamlessly with product-specific workflows
  • Working closely with engineering to navigate the technical complexities of a cross-product system
  • Presenting to and aligning senior stakeholders across multiple product lines
  • Balancing consistency and flexibility — each product has unique needs, but the whole must feel unified

Approach

The navigation redesign is structured around several core principles:

  • Product-aware, not product-specific: The navigation must understand context — which product you're in, what you're doing, and what you're likely to need next — without being locked to a single product's paradigm.
  • Progressive disclosure: Surface the most relevant navigation paths while keeping the full breadth of the ecosystem accessible. Not every user needs every product visible at all times.
  • Consistent mental model: Whether you're in Jira, Confluence, or Loom, the navigation patterns should feel familiar. Users should develop muscle memory that works across the suite.
  • Accessibility-first: Navigation is foundational infrastructure — it must work flawlessly for keyboard navigation, screen readers, and across all input modalities.

Ongoing Impact

This project is currently in progress. The navigation redesign touches every Atlassian product and requires deep coordination across product, design, and engineering teams globally. It represents one of the most significant UX infrastructure investments Atlassian has undertaken — creating a unified experience layer that ties together tools used by hundreds of thousands of organisations worldwide.
