Vodafone Unify Programme

Company Vodafone Netherlands
Role Sr. UX Designer & Programme Lead
Duration Oct 2012 – Oct 2014 (2 yrs)
Scope Consumer & Enterprise digital channels

The Challenge

Vodafone Netherlands was undergoing a major business transformation programme called 'Unify'. The company needed to redesign its digital shopping, selfcare, and support journeys for both consumer and enterprise customers. The existing digital channels had grown organically and no longer served customers effectively — leading to high call centre volumes and poor online conversion rates.

The programme required rethinking the end-to-end customer experience across web and mobile, while aligning with new backend systems and business processes being implemented simultaneously.

My Role

As Senior UX Designer and Programme Lead, I was responsible for:

  • Defining UX design processes, UX strategy, and design principles for the programme
  • Creating personas, customer journeys, storyboards, scenarios, flows, and prototypes for new digital touchpoints
  • Presenting, leading discussions, and reviews with vendors, technical architects, internal business stakeholders, and management
  • Managing design and research resources from external agencies and vendors
  • Providing guidance to business managers, product owners, and Scrum teams

Design Process

The programme spanned two years and required a structured approach to manage the complexity of simultaneous business and technology transformation:

  • Strategy & principles: Defined the UX strategy and design principles that would guide all digital touchpoint decisions across the programme
  • Customer research: Created detailed personas and mapped end-to-end customer journeys for both consumer and enterprise segments, identifying critical pain points and moments of truth
  • Concept development: Developed storyboards, scenarios, and interaction flows for all key journeys — shopping for a new plan, managing an existing subscription, troubleshooting issues, and contacting support
  • Prototyping & validation: Built prototypes across fidelity levels and ran user validation sessions to test assumptions before committing to development
  • Vendor coordination: Managed external design and research agencies, ensuring their output aligned with the programme's vision and quality standards

Key Outcomes

The Unify programme fundamentally reshaped how Vodafone Netherlands' customers interacted with the brand digitally:

  • Redesigned the complete digital shopping journey for consumer and enterprise customers
  • Created a new selfcare experience that empowered customers to manage their accounts without calling support
  • Established UX design principles and processes that continued to guide Vodafone Netherlands' digital development after the programme concluded
  • Built a cross-functional working model between UX, business, and technology that became a template for future programmes

Reflection

The Unify programme was one of the most formative experiences in my career. Working within a large-scale business transformation taught me that UX design at this level is as much about organisational design as it is about interface design. The ability to present to and influence senior stakeholders, manage external agencies, and guide Scrum teams — while keeping the customer at the centre — is a skill set that continues to serve me in my work at Atlassian today.
