About Me

I'm Arjan Helmer, a Lead Designer at Atlassian based in Sydney, Australia. For over 10 years I've been designing and leading design at Atlassian, currently redesigning the navigation experience across the entire Atlassian product suite — Jira, Confluence, Loom, Trello, Bitbucket, and more.

Before my current role, I managed the Jira Platform design team across Australia and India, and led the redesign of Jira's issue view — the most frequently used experience in the product, used by millions daily.

My career spans 17+ years across three continents — from freelance web design in Amsterdam, to research at Nokia Bell Labs in Paris, to UX leadership at Vodafone Netherlands, to where I am today. I've also served as a board member for Macheo Children's Organisation, supporting healthcare and education programmes in Kenya.

I combine hands-on design craft with strategic leadership. Having worked as an IC, a contractor, a programme lead, and a design manager, I understand the full spectrum of what it takes to ship great products.
