About Me

I'm Alex Morgan, a UX design lead and manager with over 12 years of experience building and scaling design teams across enterprise software, fintech, and healthcare.

My approach centres on empathy, evidence, and empowerment. I believe the best design leaders create environments where designers can do their most impactful work — through clear vision, strong systems, and a culture of continuous learning.

Before moving into leadership, I spent seven years as an individual contributor, working across interaction design, user research, and information architecture. That hands-on foundation informs how I mentor designers and evaluate design quality today.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me mentoring emerging designers, speaking at conferences, or exploring how AI is reshaping the design practice.
